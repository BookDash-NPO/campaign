BE PART OF THE STORY

The Next Chapter Begins With You.

You don't have to be a parent or a teacher to make a difference in a child's life. As a student or young South African, you can help make stories accessible by discovering, reading and sharing free Book Dash books.

How Can You Get Involved?

  • Explore: Discover the collection of free African children's books available on the Book Dash website.
  • Read: Enjoy a story and experience the creativity of African writers and illustrators.
  • Share: Send a free book to friends, family, teachers or community groups.
  • Spread the Word: Tell others about Book Dash and encourage them to share the joy of reading.

Ready to Make a Difference?

Every story shared is an opportunity for a child to discover something new. Take the first step and help create a future filled with books.
