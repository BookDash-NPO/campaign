MEET BOOK DASH

Creating Stories. Inspiring Young Minds.

Book Dash is a South African non-profit organisation that creates beautiful African picture books for young children. Its mission is to ensure that every child grows up with books of their own.

Our Vision

Book Dash believes that every child should own 100 books by the age of five.

How Does Book Dash Work?

  • Volunteer Creatives: Writers, illustrators and designers come together to create children's books.
  • African Stories: The books celebrate African characters, cultures and experiences.
  • Free Access: The books are shared openly so people can read, download, print, translate and share them.
  • Community Support: Partners help translate, print and distribute books to children.

Stories Should Be Shared

Book Dash makes its books available under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. This allows people to share and adapt the books while giving appropriate credit.

Together, we can help make more African stories accessible to young readers.
